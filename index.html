Our Story

Built on personality,
not just transactions.

Travel has changed. People don't just want accommodation anymore — they want authentic experiences, local connection, and a sense of community while exploring a new place.

That's what inspired the "Live Like a Local" concept. A creative outlet built on hospitality, storytelling, culture, and community-building — all the things that make a place truly memorable long after the trip is over.

What started as an idea has become something much more personal: a space where travellers, creatives, and digital nomads can share meals, stories, and genuine human connection.
